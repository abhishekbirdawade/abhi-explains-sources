Part 0

Anil Ambani was held in criminal contempt by the Supreme Court of India on 20 Feb 2019 over Rs 550 crore of Ericsson dues; on 18 March 2019, a day before the deadline, RCom paid using elder brother Mukesh's money to purge the contempt and avoid a three-month jail term.

Business Today / Business Standard (ANI)↗ev-024

It was the SUPREME COURT OF INDIA (never a 'Mumbai court'). Contempt (20 Feb) and payment (18 March) are SEPARATE dates. Mukesh's money CLEARED THE DUES to purge contempt — there was NO bail.

Business Today / Business Standard (ANI)↗ev-024

Reliance Communications' Ericsson liability was Rs 550 crore (the sum behind the 20 Feb 2019 Supreme Court contempt finding, paid 18 March 2019). The separate ~Rs 47,251 crore admitted-claims figure belongs to Reliance Communications Infrastructure Ltd and is NOT used in the narration.

Business Today / Business Standard (ANI)↗ev-024

Kumar Mangalam Birla, after the Oct 2019 AGR ruling left Vodafone Idea ~Rs 53,000 crore in dues, said the company would shut shop without relief, and in June 2021 offered the government the Aditya Birla Group's ~27% stake.

Business Standard↗ev-026

Reliance committed ~Rs 1.5 lakh crore pre-launch capex; spectrum ~Rs 13,672 crore.

source↗ev-003, ev-013

Jio ARPU Rs 181.7 (Mar 2024) to Rs 206.2 (Q4 FY25); July 2024 tariff hike 12-25%; ~33.6 GB/user/month.

source↗ev-007, ev-018, ev-008

Part 3 — free का बिल किसने भरा? Aircel के 6,000 लोग और खुद Anil Ambani।

Aircel filed for bankruptcy (NCLT, early 2018); when it collapsed ~6,000 employees had not been paid their February salary. This is a HEADCOUNT only — no ordinary laid-off worker may be named or composited (RISK [5]).

Business Today / Business Standard↗ev-025

Pre-Jio year industry combined pre-tax loss ~Rs 17bn rose to Rs 381.53bn in FY17 (TRAI) — a ~22x jump.

Business Standard↗ev-037

24 Oct 2019 Supreme Court AGR verdict landed ~Rs 1.47 lakh crore on legacy operators — Bharti Airtel ~Rs 42,000 crore, Vodafone Idea ~Rs 40,000 crore; Jio owed effectively nothing.

Business Today / Business Standard↗ev-028

Part 4 — Meter On: जब सस्ते का बिल आना शुरू हुआ।

December 2019 Jio raised prices first: 'All-in-One' plans from Rs 199, some hikes ~39%.

Meta newsroom / Business Today↗ev-029

भाग पाँच — आदत ही असली moat, फिर $20 billion का cash-out।

Facebook paid Rs 43,574 crore ($5.7bn) for 9.99%; Google Rs 33,737 crore ($4.5bn) for 7.7%; 13 investors, ~$20.2bn, ~33% of Jio Platforms in 2020.

source↗ev-005, ev-006

19 June 2026 Jio Platforms filed DRHP; IPO reported >Rs 30,000 crore, India's largest public issue; base ~488M (Mar 2025) crossing 500M.
